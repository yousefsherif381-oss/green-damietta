What is Green Technology?

🌱 Technology & Environment

Green technology encompasses solutions and practices designed to protect the environment and improve sustainability, such as energy conservation, waste management, and renewable energy usage.


Information technology itself becomes more sustainable through energy efficiency, eco-friendly hardware, and optimized data centers.
